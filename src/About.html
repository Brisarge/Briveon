I've worked on a variety of projects that showcase my ability to blend technical complexity with engaging gameplay. One of my most ambitious projects, Rhythm Rush, is a rhythm-based racing obstacle course where platforms move dynamically to the beat of the music. It utilizes procedural generation, polyrhythmic platform timing, and real-time color synchronization based on audio playback loudness. The game's challenge comes not just from jumping across moving platforms, but from mastering the rhythm itself—creating an experience that feels both intuitive and exhilarating. The backend features advanced tweening logic, randomized difficulty scaling, and a structured modular system for adding new level variations seamlessly."

Beyond Rhythm Rush, I’ve worked on a sci-fi space mining game featuring over 40 procedurally generated planet types, each with unique terrain, hazards, and rare resources. I’ve also developed a card battle game where battles take place in 3D world space rather than traditional UI, bringing cards to life with fluid animations and physics-based combat. Across all my projects, I focus on performance optimization, clean code architecture, and immersive mechanics that keep players engaged. Whether it’s implementing AI behaviors, creating modular systems for scalability, or optimizing network performance, I’m always looking for ways to push my skills and make every project feel unique.
