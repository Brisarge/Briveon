03 / About

Hey, I'm Brighton

A systems-driven creator with a love for game development, simulation, and crafting immersive digital experiences.

I've been building things since I was a kid, from wiring contraptions in GMod's Wiremod and scripting E2 chips to building AI-controlled war machines in From the Depths with modular weapon systems and drag-optimized hulls.

I thrive in games that reward problem-solving and leadership. Whether commanding squads in Hell Let Loose, running platoons in Planetside 2, or mentoring new bounty hunters in Elite: Dangerous, I naturally step into roles that require clarity, quick decision-making, and systems thinking under pressure.

That same mindset carries into my work: designing procedural systems, translating rhythm patterns into visual experiences, and shipping polished, technically ambitious games. Whether it's gameplay mechanics, real-time audio-reactive visuals, or modular tween architectures, I take pride in building things that are both engineered and expressive.
